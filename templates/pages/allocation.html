How cost is distributed

Loaded cost
Posted wages, premiums, employer taxes, benefits, insurance and agency billing for the operator
Applicable rate
Straight-time cost spreads over all paid hours; overtime premiums go only to overtime hours, derived from paid attendance under the assigned pay policy
Category cost
Hours in the category × applicable loaded rate, in exact cents
Slow-pace time
Direct hours beyond standard hours for the lines completed that day; wages are not changed
Not in time
Paid leave and agency fees stay as their own lines; unpaid meals carry no cost
